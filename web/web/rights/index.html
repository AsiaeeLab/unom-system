Questionnaires and criteria

See Questionnaire sources for the patient app's full instrument and translation attributions.

  • RECOVER-Adult Long COVID Research Index: points and threshold from Geng et al., JAMA 2025; symptom wording from the RECOVER questionnaire reported by Thaweethai et al., JAMA 2023. The publications and RECOVER materials keep their rights.
  • DePaul Symptom Questionnaire post-exertional malaise short form: Cotler et al., Diagnostics 2018, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.
  • Post-COVID-19 Functional Status scale, version 2: Klok et al., European Respiratory Journal 2020, under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 license.
  • Condition self-checks, written for this app from the National Academies 2024 Long COVID definition, the Institute of Medicine 2015 ME/CFS criteria, the 2016 revised fibromyalgia criteria, and CDC guidance on Lyme disease testing and diagnosis.
  • Lupus criteria to check: the 2019 EULAR/ACR classification criteria for systemic lupus erythematosus.

Data

  • NIH All of Us Research Program, Registered Tier, Curated Data Repository version 9 (aggregate counts only).
  • CDC National Center for Health Statistics, Household Pulse Survey, Post-COVID Conditions (data.cdc.gov dataset gsea-w83j).
  • CDC Lyme disease surveillance data, 2015 to 2019.
