Patterns in my record

Invisible illnesses such as Long COVID, ME/CFS, Lyme disease, and lupus are often diagnosed late. A health record can show patterns that are common in people diagnosed with these conditions.

This is not built yet. It would use the patient-access connections that the 21st Century Cures Act requires health systems to offer, so you could bring in your own record.
